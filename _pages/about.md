Hello! I am an Assistant Professor in the <a href="https://ceae.ku.edu/" target="_blank">Department of Civil, Environmental, and Architectural Engineering</a> and, by courtesy, the <a href="https://eecs.ku.edu/" target="_blank">Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science</a> at the University of Kansas. My research focuses on transportation cyber-physical systems, where computation and physical infrastructure are integrated at all levels. I work at the intersection of transportation engineering, control systems, and optimization, leveraging methodologies from control theory, operations research, and data science to address transportation challenges and help build safer, more efficient, and more resilient cities. [📝 View my CV](assets/docs/CV_SW.pdf)

I earned my Ph.D. in civil engineering from the University of Minnesota, where I worked with <a href="https://sites.google.com/umn.edu/mlevin/home" target="_blank">Michael Levin</a>. Prior to that, I received an M.S. in electrical and computer engineering from the University of Ottawa under the supervision of <a href="https://www.uottawa.ca/faculty-engineering/school-electrical-engineering-computer-science/directory/nasir-uddin-ahmed" target="_blank">N.U. Ahmed</a>. Outside of research, I enjoy running (5K and 10K), playing tennis, hiking, watching documentaries, and learning new card tricks.
